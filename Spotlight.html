Enginefall (Upcoming)
▶ Video
Enginefall is a new type of crafting multiplayer shooter. Board a colossal megatrain with your crew at the back, scavenge and build compact makeshift bases, push forward for better gear, then retreat to your Dagger shuttle to grow stronger and climb the train classes. Enginefall takes place entirely on colossal moving trains. From cramped tail slums to lavish first-class cars and guarded engine rooms, every carriage is a living space to loot, fight over, turn into a base, or eventually claim as part of your own rolling home as you climb from tailie to conductor. Enginefall is built around human opponents and allies. Raid trains with friends, defend your makeshift bases, and clash with other players over key routes and resources, with every encounter having the potential to flip from cooperation to conflict in a heartbeat.
Is this a game or a Social Experiment? I expect this game will be perfect for Competative Clans and Communitys, clearly inspired by Snowpiercer but with a gritty cyberpunk aesthetic almost Necromunda in style, they do have the odd playtest give it a wishlist and check out there discord.

VEIN (Recommended Game)
▶ Video
VEIN is a post-apocalyptic survival multiplayer sandbox game. In the near future, the apocalypse has occurred: hostile zombies roam the Earth, most people have died, and society has collapsed. You are a survivor. Gather supplies to survive, explore abandoned buildings, combat bandits, defend your home, and rebuild society with friends. As time passes and seasons change, the world of Vein does too. Persistent, long-scale random events affect gameplay. AI can see, hear, feel, and smell you, and react intelligently to those senses. You're being watched. Terrifying zombie variants make survival that much more difficult.
Having Played around 100hrs myself, VEIN bucks the trend of early access open world survival craft games with weekly patches and a developer who listens to the community while the games is still early and aesthetically is rough around the edge's (particularly the character creation) the main focus has been on optimzation and bug fixes if the developer keeps to his current path the game will easily surpass alternative indie titles in this niche. One aspect of the game this isn't mentioned on the steam page sandbox mode is completely customizable from the amount of loot, the percentage between running zombies and walkers, how long before water and electricity runs out to What season you start in. If you want to play in a winter hellscape with hoards of running zombies or have a peaceful world to explore that is left to the players discretion.
Rating: ★★★★☆

Join Us (Upcoming)
▶ Video
HAVE YOU EVER DREAMED OF STARTING YOUR OWN CULT? Now you can - right from the comfort of your own home! Imagine the respect you’ll receive as an up-and-coming cult leader! Issue weird teachings to your followers! Expand your territory! Build a base of operations! Perform sacrifices! Oversee a growing stockpile of military-grade weapons! In this sandbox survival game, your most valuable resource isn’t stones and wood… it’s human beings.  Every character in the world, from civilians to enemies, is a potential recruit for your growing cult. Turn the game world into your personal follower-farm by spreading propaganda, managing infamy, and making your cult worthy of joining. Once a new follower joins your flock, you can put them to work, play as them, or enlist their support on missions. All of your followers have strengths and weaknesses, and are best used in accordance with their abilities – farmers, smooth-talkers, doctors, etc.  Followers are your most important resource, but also your most volatile. If you don’t keep them happy, they’ll quit the cult. And remember… a cult leader without any followers is just a lonely weirdo. 
Whoever designed this game has clearly Spent time in a competative gaming clan, frankly this Dark comedy fusion of co-op survival and colony sim looks a lot of fun and is maybe flying under the radar for many gamers well worth wishlisting and keeping an eye on.
